how to fold one

print on US letter with your printer’s normal settings. no trimming needed: the folds line up on the paper itself. the top row prints upside down on purpose.

  1. fold the sheet into 8 rectangles: in half, in half, in half again. crease hard, then unfold.

  2. fold it in half left to right. starting at the fold, cut along the dashed line, one panel wide.

  3. unfold, fold it top to bottom, then push the ends toward the middle so the cut opens up.

  4. keep pushing until it collapses into a book. fold it shut, cover on the front. done!

a little wonky is fine. that's half the charm.
