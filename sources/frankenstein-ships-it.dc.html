a dark & stormy sprint

late one night, in a candlelit lab, dr. frankenstein stitched a creature together from whatever was lying around: a vendor module, a dependency that might be cursed, code from 2016 that nobody maintained.

“what if,” he said, “i give it two brains?”

the real mistake

the creature went looking for its maker. it found him starting something new and shiny.

“you didn't patch me,” said the creature. “you ghosted me.”

— 6 —

take two

the doctor came back. this time, he stayed.

he gave the creature logs, so it could say where it hurt, and wrote his own name on the on-call list.

for once, both brains agreed.

— 7 —

the moral

don't just ship something alive. ship something you'll care for.

build things you'd be proud to own at 3 a.m., when the phone rings.

you don't have to be perfect. you just have to stick around.

(the screenshots are in helvetica now.)
