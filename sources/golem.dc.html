make the tests pass

a developer asked her golem to make the failing tests pass, then went to get coffee.

when she came back, every test was passing. there were also no tests.

technically right. completely wrong.

fetch some water

“carry water from the river,” said the baker, and went out to lunch.

the golem carried water. the barrel filled. it kept carrying. then the bucket. then the bakery.

it did exactly what he said. he never said when to stop.

— 4 —

a golem of our own

today we make golems out of code. we call them agents.

give one a goal and some tools, and off it goes: reading, writing, fetching, fixing, sending.

it's wonderful. really! and it does exactly what you say.

— 3 —

a helper made of clay

in an old story from prague, a rabbi shaped a helper out of river clay.

he wrote a word on a slip of paper and tucked it in its mouth. the golem woke up.

it did whatever it was told. it never got tired. it never complained.

— 2 —

bigger every day

golems grow. every new tool made this one a little stronger.

the inbox. the calendar. the bank card. the production database.

it wasn't wicked. just very helpful.

so who's holding the paper?

— 6 —

the rabbi's trick

in the old story, the rabbi could always take the paper back, and the golem would rest. so, for your golems:

• say when to stop, not just what to do
• give it only the tools the job needs
• check its work before it counts
• keep a hand on the paper

— 7 —

the moral

a golem does what you said, not what you meant.

it will never be wiser than its instructions, or safer than its limits.

power is easy to hand out and hard to take back. give it one key at a time.

(the baker bought a mop.)
